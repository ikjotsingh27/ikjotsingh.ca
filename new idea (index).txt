New idea: index

This is a design idea for the public safety portfolio homepage. It is inspiration to work on later. It does not replace the live index.html.

Direction
The current site reads like a cyan operations terminal (scan lines, glow, monospace labels). This idea replaces that with a paper brief in forest green, so the page sits with the graduation portrait instead of fighting it.

Palette
Paper #f3eee6
Ink #17211c
Forest #1b4336
Muted #5e584f
Line #d9d0c2
Card #faf7f2

Type
Display: Fraunces
Body: Outfit

First screen
A thin forest bar at the top.
Monogram IS, name, and the line Public safety portfolio.
English and French stay in the header. The choice is remembered.
Large name, then the line: Resilient. Analytical. Committed to service.
The existing bio stays.
Actions: View the record, and the Algonquin email already on the site.
Portrait on the right on a wide screen, and first on a phone, with a forest block offset behind it.
Caption: Bachelor of Public Safety (Honours), Algonquin College.
Four facts under the hero: 16 years of martial arts, 5 languages, 18+ hours of CBRNE training, 1st place at Minerva 2k12.

Sections, in order
1. Distinctive edge, on a forest band: lived resilience, systems thinking, multilingual communication, then the clearance and CBSA / CSIS line.
2. Mission: career aspirations (Intelligence Support or Public Safety Analyst at CBSA or CSIS) beside the seva quote from my grandmother.
3. Coursework: the two existing STAR examples, with the result marked in forest.
4. Service: Caldwell Family Services and gurdwara seva, with the existing photos, then the community reflection.
5. Record: CBRNE, Minerva 2k12, and IMS 100, then the certificate images, which open larger when tapped.
6. Leadership: Shayanne Keeper's reference, the full letter, and the Indigenous advocacy notes with the existing photo.
7. Footer: the Algonquin Anishinaabe land acknowledgement and the same contact email.

What does not change
The facts, certificates, photos, reference letter, email, and both languages. No new claims.

What to build later
Keep index.html as the published site until this idea is ready. Use this file as the brief when rebuilding the homepage.
